Everything You Need to Know

Is Camara really 100% free? Are there hidden fees?

Yes! Camara runs entirely in your browser using modern WebGL, Web Audio, and Canvas technologies. There are no subscriptions, no locked features, and no credit card requirements.

Will my exported video have a watermark?

No. You get completely clean, watermark-free videos ready to upload to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Vimeo, or film festivals.

Can I monetize my videos on YouTube or use them for commercial clients?

Yes. All visual outputs and procedural soundtracks generated by Camara are 100% royalty-free and safe for commercial monetization without copyright claims.

Do I need to install software or download huge apps?

No installation required. It works directly inside your browser on Windows, Mac, Linux, and Chromebooks.

How does video export work?

Camara uses the HTML5 MediaRecorder and Canvas capture API to render your visual scenes and audio tracks into a standard WebM / MP4 video file saved straight to your computer's Downloads folder.

What formats and resolutions are supported?

You can create widescreen 16:9 cinema, 9:16 vertical reels/TikToks, 2.39:1 anamorphic movies, and 1:1 social square videos at up to 4K resolution.
